Customer communication policy

Privacy Policy

NJ Plumbing uses customer information to schedule and perform requested plumbing services and to provide related customer care.

Information we collect

We may collect customer names, service addresses, phone numbers, appointment details, work-order information, service notes, and communication preferences. When a customer agrees to receive text messages, we also record the date, method, disclosure version, and staff member associated with that consent.

How information is used

  • Schedule and perform requested plumbing work.
  • Provide estimates and customer-service support.
  • Send consented appointment reminders and service updates.
  • Maintain operational, safety, accounting, and compliance records.

Mobile information and SMS consent

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message originator opt-in data and consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Vendors that provide communications, hosting, data storage, or other operational services may process information solely to provide those services to NJ Plumbing and are not permitted to use it for their own marketing.

Service partners

Some work orders may originate from a service partner. NJ Plumbing does not assume that permission granted to another company authorizes NJ Plumbing text messages. NJ Plumbing obtains and documents its own permission before sending automated service texts.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide services, meet operational and legal obligations, document consent, and honor opt-out requests. Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect customer records.

Customer choices

Customers may decline SMS and continue receiving scheduling support by phone. Reply STOP to opt out of automated messages or call (888) 915-7908. Reply HELP for messaging assistance.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated to reflect operational or legal changes. The effective date below identifies the current version.
