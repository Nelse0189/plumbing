Customer communication policy

Transactional SMS consent process

NJ Plumbing uses text messaging only for service-related communication requested by customers, including scheduling, appointment reminders, arrival windows, and technician status updates.

Customers may decline text messages and continue scheduling by phone. NJ Plumbing does not send an automated text until the customer has affirmatively agreed to receive these service-related messages.

How customers opt in

1. Customer requests service

The customer calls NJ Plumbing directly or requests water-heater service through a service partner. A dispatcher or plumber calls the customer to schedule the work.

2. Staff requests permission

During the scheduling call, staff read the verbal consent script below. Consent is separate from scheduling and is not a condition of receiving service.

3. Customer makes a choice

Only a clear “yes” authorizes transactional texts. Silence, providing a phone number, or agreeing to the appointment does not count as SMS consent.

4. Consent is documented

Staff record the customer’s choice, phone number, timestamp, employee, collection method, and consent-script version in the scheduling record.

Exact verbal script

“May NJ Plumbing send you automated text messages about scheduling, reminders, and arrival windows for this job? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Consent is not required to receive service. Is that okay?”

Simulated scheduling conversation

Dispatcher: We can schedule your water-heater installation for Tuesday. May NJ Plumbing send you automated text messages about scheduling, reminders, and arrival windows for this job? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Consent is not required to receive service. Is that okay?

Customer: Yes, that is okay.

Dispatcher: Thank you. I have recorded your permission. Your first message will identify NJ Plumbing and explain how to stop or get help.

Message content and frequency

  • Messages are limited to the customer’s requested plumbing job: scheduling, reminders, arrival windows, and service updates.
  • Message frequency varies according to the active appointment.
  • No marketing or promotional content is included in this program.
  • Customers can reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for assistance.

Example production message

NJ Plumbing: Your water-heater installation arrival window is 8 AM–12 PM on August 12. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.

Consent revocation

STOP, CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, or any other reasonable request to stop is honored. Once consent is withdrawn, NJ Plumbing sends no additional automated messages except a single opt-out confirmation. Customers can still receive scheduling service by phone.
