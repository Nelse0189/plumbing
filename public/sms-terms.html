Customer communication policy

SMS Terms and Conditions

These terms govern NJ Plumbing’s transactional text-message program for customers who affirmatively request service-related messages.

Program description

NJ Plumbing may send scheduling messages, appointment confirmations, arrival-window reminders, technician status updates, and other information directly related to a plumbing service requested by the customer. This program does not include marketing or promotional messages.

Consent

Messages are sent only after the customer gives prior express consent through the process described on our SMS Consent Process page. Consent is not a condition of purchasing or receiving service. Customers who decline SMS can schedule and receive updates by phone.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on the customer’s active appointment. Message and data rates may apply according to the customer’s wireless plan.

Opt out

Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time. NJ Plumbing also honors reasonable opt-out requests made by phone or through other supported communication channels. A single confirmation message may be sent after an opt-out; no further automated messages will be sent unless the customer later provides new consent.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance or call SMS customer care at (860) 543-9082.

Supported carriers

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to carrier network availability.

Privacy

Personal and mobile information is handled according to our Privacy Policy. Mobile opt-in information and consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Changes

NJ Plumbing may update these terms as operational or legal requirements change. The effective date shown below identifies the current version.
